Our relationship

Bukerepo is a tech innovation center of Akuoro Restore Hope Initiative.

Akuoro Restore Hope Initiative (RHOI-Uganda) is a community-based nonprofit organization rooted in Serere District, Uganda, working to restore hope and expand opportunities for vulnerable communities.

Bukerepo is its Kampala-based technology skilling and innovation center. Individuals supported by Akuoro are sent from Serere to Kampala to gain, strengthen and apply practical technology skills.
