Privacy at a glance

Otucopi is designed to work without an internet connection, account, or login. According to the app's intended design, Bukerepo does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information through gameplay. Puzzle progress and personal best times are kept on the device.

1. Information we collect

Otucopi does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, or other personal details to play. It does not offer account registration or sign-in.

The app is designed without advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated servers. Bukerepo does not receive your puzzle answers, scores, progress, or completion times through the app.

If you choose to email Bukerepo directly, the email address, message, and any information you voluntarily include will be received through your email provider and used only to respond to your inquiry. Sending an email is optional and takes place outside normal gameplay.

2. Information stored on your device

To make gameplay convenient, Otucopi may save the following game information locally:

  • Unlocked and completed puzzle levels.
  • Completion times and personal-best records.
  • Other basic gameplay preferences, if supported by the installed version.

This information is used to show your progress, allow you to replay levels, and remember your achievements. It is not intended to be sent to Bukerepo or to other players.

3. Data sharing, advertising and third parties

Bukerepo does not sell or share gameplay information with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies. Otucopi does not include in-game advertisements, social sign-in, or online leaderboards.

Distribution platforms and device operating systems, such as Google Play and Android, operate under their own privacy policies. Their independent processing of information is not controlled by Bukerepo. If you contact us by email, your email provider also processes that communication under its own terms.

4. Device permissions and connectivity

Otucopi works offline and does not require any special device permissions to play. It does not ask you to grant access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or other sensitive device features. You can play without approving any Android runtime permission prompts.

Any permissions present in a particular release must match this description. Bukerepo will update this policy before introducing features that materially change device access or data handling.

5. Data security

Gameplay progress is stored in the app's local device storage rather than on a Bukerepo server. The app relies on Android's application sandbox and device security features to help protect local data. We do not maintain a remote database of player profiles or game records.

No method of device storage can be guaranteed completely secure. We recommend keeping your device software updated and using an appropriate screen lock.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally saved progress and personal-best times are retained on your device while the app and its saved data remain available. You can remove local game data through Android's app settings by clearing Otucopi's storage, or by uninstalling the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently erase your progress; Android backup or device-transfer features, if enabled, may affect whether local data can later be restored.

Because Bukerepo does not maintain player accounts or remotely store gameplay records, there is no Bukerepo gameplay account or server-side progress record to delete. For deletion questions or optional email correspondence, contact us using the details below.

7. Age suitability

Otucopi is intended for individuals aged 13 and above. The game does not require users to disclose their age, and it does not intentionally collect personal information from children or teenagers through gameplay.

The stated intended audience does not replace Google Play's separate target-audience and content-rating declarations. Those declarations are made in Google Play Console based on the actual app and intended distribution.

8. Changes to this policy

Bukerepo may revise this Privacy Policy if the app's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be posted at the public privacy-policy URL with a revised effective date. Material changes to information handling will be described before the updated practices take effect, as required by applicable law and platform rules.
